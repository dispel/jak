Supported platforms
===================

Python
------

jak is explicitly tested on Pythons:

- 3.9
- 3.10
- 3.11
- 3.12
- 3.13
- 3.14
- 3.15
- PyPy3

jak follows the `Python end of support dates <https://devguide.python.org/versions/>`_. 3.9 and 3.10 are past their end of support and will be dropped in a future release.

Operating Systems
--

We believe jak should work well on most `*nix <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix-like>`_ systems. But is mainly developed on Ubuntu and tested on Ubuntu and macOS.
